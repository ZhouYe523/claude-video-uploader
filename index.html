Claude Video Uploader

A personal upload tool for one YouTube channel

Claude Video Uploader is a small script that runs on its owner's own Windows PC. When the channel owner asks, Claude (Anthropic's AI assistant) uses it to upload finished videos to the owner's YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API: the video file, its title, description, tags, privacy setting, thumbnail and subtitle tracks.

It is not a public service. Only the channel owner's own Google account is authorised to use it, and nothing is uploaded unless the owner asks for it.
